# User Guide

This guide explains how to open and navigate the Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard. It is written for a business audience with no technical background required.

## Opening the dashboard

Open the Tableau Package Workbook (`.twbx`) file in Tableau Reader or Tableau Desktop. Once it opens, you will see the full dashboard on one screen.

The layout is organized top to bottom:

* Two KPI summary boxes at the top: Total Customers and Overall Churn Rate
* Two main charts in the middle: Churn Rate by State and Churn by Contract Type
* Two supporting charts below that: Churn Rate by Age Group and Churn Rate versus Customer Contacts
* Filters along the side

This layout lets you examine churn patterns by who is leaving, where they are located, and what factors are connected to it, all in one view.

## Using Filter 1: Contract Type

Find the Contract filter on the right side of the dashboard. Click the dropdown arrow and select one or more contract types. The dashboard updates automatically, and every chart and KPI recalculates to reflect only the selected contract type or types. Use this to focus on churn patterns for Month to Month, One Year, Two Year, or all customers together.

## Using Filter 2: Area

Find the Area filter on the left side of the dashboard. Click the dropdown arrow and select one or more area categories. The dashboard updates automatically, recalculating every chart and KPI to reflect only the selected area group or groups. Use this to compare churn patterns across different customer regions.

## What you can learn from it

Using the two filters together, a viewer can identify which contract types have the highest churn, which states show elevated churn rates, how churn differs across age groups, and whether repeated customer contact correlates with churn. The goal is to let anyone compare these groups and spot patterns without needing a background in data analysis.
