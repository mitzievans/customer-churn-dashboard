# Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard that helps telecom executives explore customer churn, spot the segments most at risk, and act on retention opportunities, no data background required.

## Overview

Acquiring a new telecom customer costs far more than keeping an existing one, yet churn erodes revenue every quarter. This dashboard turns raw customer level data into a single view that a business leader can explore on their own: who is leaving, where, and what is correlated with it.

## Features

* Two KPIs for total customers and overall churn rate
* Four visualizations covering geography, contract type, age group, and support contact frequency
* Two interactive filters (contract type and customer area) that update every chart and KPI at once
* A colorblind accessible palette

## Approach

The dashboard is built around a problem, insight, action structure. The KPIs establish the scale of churn, the charts show where and why it concentrates, and the filters let a viewer test their own hypotheses by segment rather than reading a static report. Color and placement are used deliberately to draw attention to the highest value findings first, and the same underlying data supports both a quick executive glance and a deeper technical pass, presented differently depending on the audience.

## View it

Open `dashboard/customer_churn_dashboard.twbx` in Tableau Desktop or the free Tableau Reader to explore it interactively.
